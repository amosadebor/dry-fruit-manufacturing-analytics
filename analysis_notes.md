# Analysis Notes

## Analytical workflow

1. Frame the business question.
2. Understand the row structure and measures.
3. Validate data quality and KPI inputs.
4. Calculate core production, quality and downtime KPIs.
5. Segment performance by machine, shift, product and month.
6. Reproduce key analyses in SQL.
7. Test relationships and interactions.
8. Stress-test important relationships across subgroups.
9. Translate evidence into investigation priorities.
10. Communicate results through management-oriented dashboards.

## Core analytical principles

- Start with the business question rather than the chart.
- Use segmented analysis where aggregate results can conceal operational differences.
- Treat correlation as evidence of association, not proof of causation.
- Use diagnostic language such as **investigate**, **review**, **validate** and **prioritise** where root cause has not been established.
- Keep simulated findings explicitly separated from real employer experience.
