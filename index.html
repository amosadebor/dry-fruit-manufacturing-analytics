THE BUSINESS QUESTION

What operational factors appear to be associated with production underperformance, quality losses and HSE exposure?

I approached the problem as an operational analytics exercise rather than beginning with a predefined chart. The workflow moved from data understanding and validation through KPI construction, segmented analysis, SQL reproduction, deeper relationship testing and management-oriented dashboarding.

SELECTED SIMULATED FINDINGS
01

Downtime ↔ attainment

Overall record-level correlation is approximately -0.663. The relationship remains present after segmentation by machine and shift.

Interpretation: higher downtime observations tend to coincide with lower attainment in this simulation; this does not prove causation.

02

Dryer 3

Dryer 3 records approximately 8,668 downtime minutes and a 13.41% downtime rate, the highest simulated machine-level burden.

03

Shift C

Shift C records approximately 84.51% attainment and the highest shift-level downtime rate.

04

Shift C × Dryer 3

The strongest diagnostic combination records approximately 80.47% attainment, 15.38% downtime and 4.51% reject rate.

05

Dried Mango × Dryer 3

This product-machine cohort has the highest simulated reject rate at approximately 4.82%.

06

Weak HSE relationships

GMP/reject and PPE/safety-observation relationships are weak in the simulation, so they are retained as contextual indicators rather than causal drivers.

MANAGEMENT INVESTIGATION PRIORITIES

What the evidence suggests should be investigated next

  1. Diagnose Dryer 3 / Shift C: review event-level downtime, repeat stoppages and operating context.
  2. Improve downtime taxonomy: capture structured stop reasons, timestamps, loss categories and recurrence.
  3. Drill into Dried Mango × Dryer 3: add process conditions, defect categories and quality checkpoint data.
  4. Review Shift C operating context: validate handover, workload, escalation and action backlog.
  5. Improve HSE data granularity: add severity, exposure hours, action age, ownership and repeat-occurrence fields.

Analytical discipline

The recommendations are framed as investigations, not root-cause conclusions. Real operational decisions require validated production, maintenance, quality and HSE records.

LIMITATIONS & DISCLOSURE

Synthetic dataset

The entire working dataset is simulated. Patterns are illustrative and do not represent actual employer performance.

Association ≠ causation

Observed relationships are not treated as proof that one factor caused another.

Context gaps

The data lacks enough process-context variables to establish actual root causes.

Trend caution

July contains only nine records and should be treated cautiously in trend interpretation.
