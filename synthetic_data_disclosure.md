# Synthetic Data Disclosure

**Important:** This is a simulated manufacturing analytics case study using synthetic data.

The dataset and resulting patterns were created for portfolio learning and analytical demonstration. The findings do **not** represent actual ReelFruit performance, any other employer's performance, or the performance of any real machine, shift, product or employee group.

The analysis also distinguishes association from causation. A relationship observed in the simulation is not evidence that one operational factor caused another.

Any real operational decision would require validated production, maintenance, quality and HSE records and appropriate operational context.
